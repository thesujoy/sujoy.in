A universe written in surprisingly little ink.

Some equations are only a few symbols long, yet completely changed how we understand reality.

E = mc²
Mass–energy equivalence

Einstein showed that mass and energy are two forms of the same thing, connected by the speed of light.

F = ma
Newton's second law

The motion of an object changes according to the force acting on it and the amount of mass being moved.

E = hν
Quantum energy

Planck's relation tells us that the energy carried by light comes in discrete amounts connected to frequency.

S = k ln Ω
Boltzmann's entropy equation

A bridge between the invisible microscopic arrangements of matter and the thermodynamic world we experience.

V = IR
Ohm's law

Voltage, current and resistance are linked by one of the simplest relationships in electrical science.

Science isn't only about finding answers. Sometimes its greatest achievement is teaching us how much remains unexplained.
