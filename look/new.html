What is Relativity?

Imagine you and your friend both have a clock. If your friend stays on Earth while you travel in a spaceship moving almost as fast as light, something unbelievable happens. When you return... Your friend's clock shows more time has passed than yours. It isn't because either clock is broken. Time itself moves differently. That is one of the biggest discoveries made by Albert Einstein.


Relativity tells us that space and time are connected. The faster you move, the slower time passes for you compared with someone standing still.

Einstein's Famous Formula

E = mc²

This is probably the world's most famous equation. It tells us that matter and energy are actually two forms of the same thing.


Symbol Meaning
E Energy
m Mass
c Speed of Light (300,000,000 m/s)

Even a tiny amount of mass contains an enormous amount of energy because the speed of light is squared. That is why stars can shine for billions of years.

CHAPTER 3

🌌 General Relativity

In 1915, Einstein completely changed our understanding of gravity. He proposed that gravity is not a force pulling objects. Instead, mass bends space and time. Objects simply follow the curves of spacetime.

"Gravity is geometry."
— A simple way to describe Einstein's idea
🌍

Mass

Every object with mass bends spacetime. Bigger objects create deeper curves.

🌌

Spacetime

Space and time are connected together into one four-dimensional fabric.

🛰️

Motion

Planets are not being pulled. They naturally move along curved paths.

💡 Imagine This

Imagine placing a heavy bowling ball on a stretched rubber sheet. The sheet bends. Now roll a marble nearby. The marble curves toward the bowling ball. The sheet represents spacetime. The bowling ball represents the Sun. The marble represents Earth.

🛰️ GPS

Your phone works correctly because GPS satellites use Einstein's relativity every day.

🌠 Light

Even light bends when it passes close to a very massive object.

🕳️ Black Holes

Black holes bend spacetime so much that even light cannot escape.
